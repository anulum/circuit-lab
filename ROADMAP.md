- native documentation;
- dedicated validation tools.

**Domain capabilities and claims:** empty.

## Capability milestones

1. **The five planned v1 record schemas with golden fixtures** (`measurement_association`, `timebase_association`, `measurement_request`, `measurement_capture`, `calibration_certificate`).
2. **Calibration-expiry, timebase-skew and high-voltage probe-isolation pre-admission validators.**
3. **First-silicon bring-up, shmoo error-parity and witness-handoff adapters** for offline and human-operated bench sessions.

**Enrolled with every implementation change:**
- strict native typing and linting;
- native documentation;
- meaningful statement and branch coverage;
- affected boundary tests and benchmarks.

**Requires its own evidence and explicit approval:**
- every physical bench power-on, probing or thermal-stress action;
- destructive failure-analysis sample quarantine;
- public activation and release.

No availability date is promised. No performance, yield or productivity gain is promised either.
